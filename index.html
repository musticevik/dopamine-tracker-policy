2.3 Cihaz kullanım istatistikleri

Android'de UsageStatsManager üzerinden hangi uygulamayı ne kadar kullandığınız bilgisi yalnızca cihazınızda yerel olarak işlenir. Bu ham veri sunucularımıza gönderilmez. Yalnızca türetilmiş skorlar ve odak oturum geçmişi bulut hesabınızda saklanır.

Dopamine Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026 · Effective date: May 8, 2026

1. Data controller

This application (Dopamine Tracker, package com.agilersoft.dopaminetracker) is developed by Mustafa Çevik. Contact: agilersoft@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Email address — only for account creation and verification (email + 6-digit OTP).
  • Name and profile picture URL — only if you choose Google sign-in, provided by Google.
  • Account creation date

2.2 In-app preferences and goals

  • Your motivation choices (exam, productivity, health, etc.)
  • Daily social media limit goal
  • Goal duration (days)
  • Onboarding completion timestamp
  • Language and theme preferences

2.3 Device usage statistics

On Android, application usage data accessed through UsageStatsManager is processed locally on your device only. This raw data is never transmitted to our servers. Only derived dopamine scores and focus session history are stored in your cloud account.

2.4 Automatically processed technical data

  • Anonymous error logs (only on crashes, no personal data)
  • Anonymous session tokens for authentication

2.5 Data we do NOT collect

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Microphone / camera
  • Advertising identifiers (AdID)
  • Third-party analytics or marketing trackers

We share data with the following providers only as necessary to deliver the service:

  • Google LLC (only if you used Google sign-in) — Privacy policy

None of these providers use your data for marketing or profiling; they perform only the function for which we engaged them.

5. Data retention

  • While your account is active: all account data is retained.
  • When you delete your account: all data is permanently deleted within 30 days (the in-app "Delete my account" button deletes immediately).
  • Accounts inactive for 12 months: we send a reminder; if no response in 30 days, the account is deleted.

6. International data transfers

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Transfers outside Türkiye are made under Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Security measures

  • All data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Row-level security (RLS) at the database — each user can only access their own rows.
  • We use one-time codes (OTP) instead of passwords; no passwords stored.
  • Authentication tokens are kept in your device's local secure storage.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You always have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — instantly via "Profile → Delete my account" or by emailing us
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Send rights requests to agilersoft@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

9. Children's policy

This app is not designed for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we discover such collection, the data is deleted immediately.

10. Automated decision-making and profiling

We do not perform automated individual decision-making or profiling on your data. Score calculations happen on your device solely for personal feedback.

11. Cookies and trackers

The app does not use cookies. It does not access advertising identifiers (AdID/IDFA). No third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) are integrated.
